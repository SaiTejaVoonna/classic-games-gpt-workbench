Instructions

Guide the snake to the round fruit. Each one adds a segment and a point. Hitting a maze block or yourself costs a life. With Solid walls, hitting a wall costs a life too. In the default Classic mode, losing a life ends the run.

Bonus food: a diamond with a ring appears now and then. The ring empties as time runs out. Eating it gives extra points and does not grow the snake. Missing it costs nothing.

Edges and mazes: with Wrap around, leaving one edge brings you back on the opposite edge. Maze blocks are always solid. Choose these in Game settings before a run.

Steering: swipe on the board, use the direction buttons, or use the arrow keys or WASD while the board is focused. Space or P pauses and resumes.

The run starts with your first direction. The game pauses when the tab is hidden, and you resume it yourself.

About: An early version of Classic Games. Gameplay may differ from the originals.
